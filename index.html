The problem

Generic software misses the way salon work actually happens.

  • 1

    A stylist needs to find the next client quickly, without digging through menus.

  • 2

    The last formula must be easy to retrieve—right before the client sits down.

  • 3

    Processing time should not automatically create false scheduling conflicts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Cosmofy a client booking app?

No. Cosmofy is a private business and record-management app controlled by the stylist. It is not a consumer booking marketplace, and clients do not book their own appointments through it.

Can clients see my calendar?

No. Clients do not access or control the calendar. The stylist stays in charge of scheduling.

Does Cosmofy require a subscription?

Pricing and release details will be announced before launch.

Is Cosmofy available for iPhone?

The current product is Android.

Where is salon information stored?

Records are designed to be stored locally on the stylist’s Android device.

Can I export my records?

Yes. User-initiated backup and export are supported.

Is Cosmofy already on Google Play?

No. Cosmofy is currently in a small, invite-only Android beta and is not yet publicly listed on Google Play. This page will be updated if that changes.

How can I join beta testing?

Use the “Contact Cosmofy” button above to reach out. Space is limited to a small, approved group of testers, so not everyone who applies will be included.
