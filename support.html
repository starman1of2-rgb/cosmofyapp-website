Support

Cosmofy™ is available now on Google Play. Here's how to get help, install it, and find pricing.

Current status

Available now on Google Play

Cosmofy is published and publicly available on Google Play in the United States. Get Cosmofy on Google Play (opens in a new tab).

Installing and updating

Search for “Cosmofy Salon Manager” on Google Play, or use the direct link above. Select the displayed price or Buy button, complete the Google Play purchase, and then install the app. After installation, Google Play delivers updates automatically unless you’ve disabled automatic updates for Cosmofy.

Join the Cosmofy™ Community

For general questions, tips, feature ideas, and real-world salon discussion, join the Cosmofy™ Community on Facebook (opens in a new tab).

Contact support

For account-specific issues, bugs, or private matters, Email Support.

Please do not email or post: client names or contact information, client formulas, private client photographs, financial exports, backup files, or passwords. Support requests should describe the issue, not include private salon data.

Helpful information to include

  • Android version
  • Phone model
  • App version (if installed)
  • A general description of the issue
