Support

Cosmofy™ is now in Android beta testing. Here's how to get help and what to expect.

Current status

Now in Android beta testing

Cosmofy has entered real-world Android beta testing with a small, approved group of independent stylists and cosmetology professionals. It is not yet publicly listed on the Google Play Store. This page will be updated when that changes.

Approved testers are expected to install and use the app in their real salon work, report problems, and provide useful feedback. As a thank-you, testers who actively participate will receive the released Cosmofy app at no cost — beta participation doesn’t include cash compensation, hardware, future paid services, or unlimited future products.

Beta software may still contain defects. Use sample or noncritical data until you’re comfortable with how the app behaves.

Installing and updating

During the beta period, installation and update instructions will be provided directly to beta participants. General public installation guidance will be published here once a public listing exists.

Contact support

Email Support with questions or issues.

Please do not email: client names or contact information, client formulas, private client photographs, financial exports, backup files, or passwords. Support requests should describe the issue, not include private salon data.

Helpful information to include

  • Android version
  • Phone model
  • App version (if installed)
  • A general description of the issue

Common questions

When will Cosmofy be available?

Cosmofy is currently in a small, approved Android beta. Pricing and public release details will be announced before wider release.

How do I join the beta?

Use the "Contact Cosmofy" button on the homepage to apply. Space is limited to a small, approved group of testers, so not everyone who applies will be included.
