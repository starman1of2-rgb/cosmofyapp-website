Accessibility statement

Cosmofy is designed to support Android accessibility features including screen readers, scalable text, and alternate navigation methods. We continue to test and improve accessibility and welcome reports of barriers.

The Cosmofy Android app

  • Developed with screen-reader semantics in mind, including TalkBack.
  • Controls are built with meaningful labels and states.
  • Gesture-only actions, such as appointment removal, have an accessible alternative.
  • Touch targets and color contrast have been reviewed.
  • Large text and Android display-scaling settings are part of testing.
  • TalkBack was manually tested successfully on a physical Pixel device during development.
  • Accessibility is treated as an ongoing responsibility across future releases, not a one-time checkbox.

Cosmofy is not described as fully compliant with any specific accessibility law or certification. Known limitations may exist, and we welcome reports of barriers so they can be addressed.

This website

  • Semantic HTML headings in a logical order on every page.
  • A "Skip to main content" link as the first focusable element.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including the mobile menu.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators throughout.
  • Responsive text that remains usable at 200% browser zoom.
  • Support for prefers-reduced-motion to minimize animation.
  • Descriptive link text rather than generic "click here" links.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds.

Reporting a barrier

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website or in the Cosmofy app, please Email Support. Please describe the page or screen, the device and assistive technology you used, and what you expected to happen.
