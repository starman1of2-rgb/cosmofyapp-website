Privacy at Cosmofy™

This statement covers two separate things: this public website, and the Cosmofy Android app.

Last updated: August 2026. This is a plain-language statement, not an attorney-reviewed legal document.

This public website

  • The static website at cosmofyapp.com uses no analytics by default.
  • It uses no advertising trackers.
  • It sets no intentional marketing cookies.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website, may operate its own hosting infrastructure and logs independently of Cosmofy. See GitHub's own documentation for details on GitHub Pages infrastructure.
  • Clicking an email link or a future beta-signup form link may take you to another service (such as your email provider or a form provider) that operates under its own privacy practices.
  • No form data is currently stored by this website, because this website does not currently host any data-collecting forms.

The Cosmofy Android app

  • Cosmofy is designed for local salon recordkeeping on the stylist's own Android device.
  • Exports and backups are user-initiated and under the user's control.
  • App privacy details may be expanded and published in more depth before public release.

Questions

If you have a question about this privacy statement, Contact Cosmofy.
